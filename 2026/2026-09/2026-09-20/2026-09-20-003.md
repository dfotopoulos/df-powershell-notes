# 2026-09-20-003

## Summary

### Cause of the Failure

The failure was caused by evaluating `.IsPresent` on `$WhatIfPreference`:

1. In PowerShell, `$WhatIfPreference` is a `[System.Boolean]` (`$true` or
   `$false`), not a `SwitchParameter` object.
2. Under `Set-StrictMode -Version Latest`, attempting to access a non-existent
   property on a boolean (`$WhatIfPreference.IsPresent`) throws:
    ```text
    PropertyNotFoundException: The property 'IsPresent' cannot be found on this object. Verify that the property exists.
    ```
3. When running interactively without `-WhatIf`, `$WhatIfPreference` is
   `$false`. Evaluating `$false.IsPresent` triggered the exception at line 280
   when entering `Invoke-LocalAdminCreation`.

---

### Fix Applied

In
[Setup-Workstation.ps1](file:///C:/usr/src/df/powershell/df-powershell/windows/setup/Setup-Workstation.ps1):

1. **Replaced `.IsPresent` with native boolean coercion:**

    ```powershell
    # Before
    $isWhatIf = $WhatIfPreference.IsPresent -or ($PSCmdlet -and $PSCmdlet.MyInvocation.BoundParameters.ContainsKey('WhatIf'))

    # After
    $isWhatIf = [bool]$WhatIfPreference
    ```

    Applied to both `Invoke-LocalAdminCreation` (line 280) and the elevation
    check (line 457).

2. **Ensured LocalUser Object Availability:** Updated account creation to fetch
   the user via `Get-LocalUser` immediately after `New-LocalUser`, ensuring the
   object and SID are fully populated for the Administrators group addition.

---
